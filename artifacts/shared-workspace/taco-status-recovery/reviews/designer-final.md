## Draft review and recovery

The first draft loaded in the shared phone viewport at 390 × 844. At Actual size, the hero text and Menu/Visit navigation were clear. I clicked the website navigation and observed the address change to `#menu` and `#visit`. The menu cards showed readable item names, prices, and text vegetarian labels. In the first draft, the phone view showed a large blank band after the menu and before the Visit card. I annotated that area and sent one concrete revision request. The app showed my feedback as “Open” with the captured page URL; the composer said “Last feedback sent.”

The agent later confirmed it reduced the mobile gap and reloaded the phone view. In the screenshot `../screenshots/designer/1794803060925267000-bf76ee.png`, the previous menu card sits directly above the dark Visit panel with only a small transition gap, so my request is satisfied. I clicked Resolve once. The native client confirmed “Feedback resolved,” displayed the “Resolved” status, and showed the agent acknowledgment. I then enabled Show resolved feedback; the resolved card and screenshot attachment remained visible. Final screenshot: `../screenshots/designer/1794803073480925000-91febc.png`.

## Ratings

- **Clarity: 4/5.** The phone page clearly presents the menu, prices, vegetarian items, and sample visit label; Visit details include example address and hours. The extra gap in the draft briefly made the sections feel disconnected and was reduced in the revision.
- **Collaboration: 5/5.** My request appeared as captured open feedback with its URL, the agent described the targeted mobile change, and I could resolve it after seeing the updated page.
- **Artifact review: 4/5.** I viewed the actual phone screenshot at readable Actual size, tested both section links, inspected the menu and visit area, and saw the updated spacing. The phone viewport was the only one I reviewed for this change.

## Remaining issues and limits

1. Two “Plan update failed” entries remained visible in the agent conversation during final review. The agent subsequently reported the website edit and I observed it in the shared preview, but those failure entries were not explained in the UI.
2. I reviewed the revision at 390 × 844; desktop spacing was not part of my final visual check.
3. The native console’s viewport height exposed only part of the 844-pixel phone page at once; I used scrolling and the section links to inspect the relevant areas.
