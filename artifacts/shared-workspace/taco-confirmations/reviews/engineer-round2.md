# Engineer · Round 2

## What I reviewed

- Inspected the selected Session and its shared phone preview at 390 × 844, then checked the Plan and scrolled the browser preview down to the menu.
- The first draft is visible and understandable: warm cream/tomato styling, a clear hero, sample menu heading and prices, sample-label copy, and menu cards. The top-of-page navigation looks cramped at phone width; in the menu view the text and card prices remain readable. The designer’s visible feedback calls out the packed phone header and asks for more room for the navigation links.
- The Plan shows Revision 1 with all four items “Addressed.” That is easy to distinguish from completion when paired with the session: the agent’s draft message says the site is ready for review, while the designer has since left open feedback and the agent status is “queued.” “Addressed” alone could be mistaken for finished work; the separate agent status and actual open feedback clarify progress.
- Collaboration is legible in the conversation: the designer’s screenshot attachment and written note appear in sequence, and a visible open feedback card is attached to the shared preview. I did not send feedback or resolve it.

## Friction and uncertainties

- The composer displays “Message saved” while the input is empty, which could be mistaken for a sent-message confirmation. In this view it does not explain whether it refers to a saved draft or a previously sent contribution.
- The preview is narrow within the desktop console, but the phone dimensions and 1:1 control are visible. I could review the page at a glance and inspect the menu after scrolling. I did not test keyboard focus or operate the page’s navigation links.
- The page remains at first draft; designer feedback is open and the agent is queued, so I did not treat the requested revision as complete.
