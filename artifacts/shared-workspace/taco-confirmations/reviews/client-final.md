# Client final review

## Initial impression and recovery

In my first round, the selected Session had no saved items and the preview said “Demo plan — Waiting for the team’s brief.” I sent one short site brief; the bridge returned `ok: true` with “Message saved.” At that point, there was no website to review.

The first draft later appeared as a warm cream-and-tomato phone page. On final review I saw a real transient client banner, “The request timed out. Your draft is preserved.” The request’s outcome was therefore initially uncertain from the error alone. The visible conversation then showed recovery: the agent reported updating the phone header, the browser displayed the wider navigation and revised menu, Plan showed Revision 3 with 5 of 5 addressed, Maya’s feedback card said “Resolved this feedback,” and the Session returned to “Ready for the next step.” The rendered page and saved progress were present despite the timeout banner. I did not submit further feedback.

## Ratings

- **Clarity: 4/5.** The composer’s “Message saved” status confirmed my initial contribution, and the conversation plus “Ready for the next step” separated agent progress from message saving. However, the saved status remained visible with an empty composer, so it could be misread as a new save confirmation. The timeout banner also obscured part of the browser controls even though the revision had recovered.
- **Collaboration: 5/5.** The conversation summarized the team’s design/engineering direction, the Plan made the five requirements and addressed status visible, and Maya’s feedback resolution appeared in the conversation. The client showed the agent’s follow-up and the resulting update without needing a new message from me.
- **Artifact review: 4/5.** I visually reviewed the actual phone page before and after the update. The final screen shows the header links on their own row with larger targets; a later shared scroll showed the sample menu and prices. These support the requested improvement and content direction. I did not test links or verify the full page and behavior.

## Remaining issues and limits

1. The timeout banner remained visible after the page, conversation, Plan, and status showed recovery. It leaves the relationship between timeout and successful revision unclear until the user checks the other surfaces.
2. The page screenshot is a narrow phone preview and only exposes part of the page at once. I saw the hero/navigation and later a portion of the sample menu, but did not review the story or visit details in this final round.
3. I did not test taps, anchor navigation, keyboard focus, or responsive behavior at other viewport sizes. “Comfortably tappable” is supported by the visual enlargement, not a direct interaction test.
