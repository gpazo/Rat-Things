The revised draft meets the client brief as a static, fictional neighborhood taquería site. In the final revision, the biggest visible change is a decorative CSS taco replacing the abstract hero shape. 2. Confirm the compact “Sample menu” and “Visit samples” navigation targets. In my one approximate Fit-scale page click, the URL changed from `#menu` to `#top`; the coordinate may have landed on a different target, so I cannot call this a confirmed navigation fault.
3. Recheck the complete visit section and the final page at desktop size before treating responsive behavior as fully reviewed.
