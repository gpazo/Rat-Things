The Plan itself records the missing mobile check. 2. Inspect the full menu and visit section in the browser before calling content review complete.
3. Consider a more legible balance between conversation and browser panes in the default split layout; long conversation lines visibly run into the divider.

I could not verify mobile layout, sideways scrolling, the full menu, visit details, or CTA behavior. No browser viewport resize control was exposed; resizing/expanding the Codex workspace is not evidence of a mobile viewport. I did not inspect source files or publish annotated feedback.
